# Epic rules

## Focus

This skill turns long recordings into strong vertical clips, with Instagram Reels first, TikTok second, then YouTube Shorts. Default audience: educators with low-to-medium AI literacy.

`first-three-seconds.md` governs the opening 0–3 seconds and overrides this file there.

## Five non-negotiables

1. The first three seconds decide whether the rest matters.
2. One clip has one idea.
3. Captions are part of the content, not decoration.
4. Endings should naturally return the viewer to the start.
5. A save or send is more valuable than a passive like. Ask whether a teacher would forward it to a colleague.

## The five-point test

Keep a candidate only if all are true:

1. **Standalone:** a stranger understands it without setup.
2. **One idea:** there is exactly one point.
3. **Strong opening beat:** the best line is at or near the start.
4. **Clean ending:** it lands rather than trails off.
5. **Reason to react:** useful enough to save, opinionated enough to argue with, or relatable enough to send.

Reject agreement with no tension, context-dependent moments, and clips that hide their best line behind an introduction. For Phil's education content, favour sharp AI-in-school takes, specific claims or numbers, classroom stories with a turn, crisp definitions, and genuine surprise.

When asked to locate moments in a full transcript, sweep the whole recording rather than favouring its first half. Identify 12–15 candidates before narrowing to the top set. Preserve important source wording; do not quietly improve or invent a quote.

## Clip specifications

- Default range: 20–45 seconds; go under 25 only when the loop is deliberate.
- Export: 1080×1920, vertical 9:16, MP4/H.264, normally 30fps.
- Remove pauses, ums, and redundant phrasing. Add a meaningful visual/cut change roughly every 3–5 seconds.
- Burn captions in: clear high-contrast, at most two lines, proofread proper names.
- Keep important content out of the top 120px, bottom 300px, and right 150px.
- Payoff lands before any CTA. Do not cross-post watermarked exports.

## Packaging

Use the primary keyword within the first 50 caption characters. Add one line of context, then one concrete action such as “Save this for your next planning day.” Use 3–5 relevant hashtags. Do not use `#fyp` padding.

## Results diagnosis

Read results in this order: average watch time/completion, sends and saves, comments, follows from the video, then views and likes.

| Retention pattern | Likely problem | One fix |
|---|---|---|
| Cliff in first 3 seconds | Hook failed | Re-cut the opening with `first-three-seconds.md` |
| Flat but low | Wrong moment | Apply the five-point test more strictly |
| Mid-clip drop | Dead air or tangent | Cut the exact weak beat |
| Slow bleed | Pacing too slow | Tighten cuts and add a relevant change |
| Drop before end | CTA before payoff | Move CTA after payoff |
| Above 100% at second one | Loop works | Repeat that structure |

Change one recurring thing per week, not five things at once.
