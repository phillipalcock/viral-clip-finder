# Platform specs and formatting playbook

All outputs: 9:16, 1080×1920, burned-in captions, a strong first-frame thumbnail, and no important text in the top 15% or bottom 20%.

## TikTok

- Sweet spot: 15–34 seconds; longer only for a strong story.
- Hook in 1–2 seconds. Use visible on-screen text and word-level emphasis.
- Keep dialogue audible under any music.
- Caption: short, casual, with a specific question where appropriate.
- Use 3–5 relevant hashtags, not `#fyp` padding.

## Instagram Reels

- Sweet spot: 7–30 seconds and built for a clean loop.
- First frame must work as cover art; visual and text hook together.
- Caption begins with clear value, then a save/share action.
- Use up to five relevant hashtags.

## YouTube Shorts

- Sweet spot: 20–60 seconds; a little more context is acceptable.
- State the payoff early, verbally and visually.
- Use a searchable curiosity-led title.
- Keep captions clear of faces; original dialogue is usually enough.
- Use 2–3 hashtags including `#Shorts` where useful.

One idea per clip. If there are two ideas, cut two clips. End after the payoff with a natural loop or comment invitation.
