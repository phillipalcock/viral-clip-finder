# Video input guide

## Best input

1. A timestamped transcript, SRT, or VTT — enables precise cuts.
2. A plain transcript — timestamps must be estimated.
3. A detailed scene description, key quotes, and runtime — only rough moments can be suggested.

## Quick prompts

- `Use viral-clip-finder on this podcast transcript. Find five TikTok and Reels clips.`
- `Analyse this and give me the three highest-scoring YouTube Shorts.`
- `This is a 40-minute interview. Pull the most shareable 30-second moments.`

## Limits

Trend and audio advice is directional unless the model has verified live data. Verify sounds and current formats in the native app before posting.
