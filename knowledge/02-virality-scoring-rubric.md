# Virality scoring rubric

Score each candidate out of 100.

| Factor | Maximum | What earns the score |
|---|---:|---|
| Hook strength | 25 | Immediate verbal/visual pull in seconds 0–3 |
| Emotional intensity | 20 | One strong emotion: awe, laughter, surprise, anger, relatability |
| Shareability | 15 | Worth sending, saving, or using to signal identity |
| Relatability | 15 | Broad relevance or a strongly activated niche |
| Completeness | 10 | Standalone story and satisfying payoff |
| Length fit | 10 | Tight, platform-appropriate pacing |
| Rewatch / loop | 5 | Detail, clean loop, or a reason to replay |

## Bands

- **85–100:** Priority post.
- **70–84:** Strong.
- **55–69:** Decent but needs a stronger hook or tighter cut.
- **Below 55:** Skip or heavily rework.

When tied, prefer stronger hook, then emotion, then a shorter/tighter evergreen clip.

Cap a score when a clip needs external context, starts slowly, never reaches its payoff, or relies on unexplained jargon.

Use this exact display: `Score 88/100 — Hook 24 | Emotion 18 | Share 13 | Relate 13 | Complete 9 | Length 8 | Rewatch 3`.
